Trade Compliance Framework

Interactive Incoterms® Matrix (Pure CSS Tabs)

FOB — Free On Board (Named Port of Shipment)

The seller delivers the goods loaded on board the vessel at the export port. Risk transfers to the buyer the moment goods pass the ship rail.

Seller 40%
Buyer 60%
Hunain's Operational Scope: Negotiates ocean freight contracts with carrier lines, books cargo space, audits original B/L release, and oversees terminal clearance at Karachi.

CIF — Cost, Insurance and Freight (Destination Port)

The seller pays for freight and minimum marine insurance coverage to Karachi port. Risk passes when goods are loaded at origin.

Seller 75%
Buyer 25%
Hunain's Operational Scope: Audits seller insurance policy adequacy, manages customs duty calculations in WeBOC, and expedites Delivery Order (D/O) issuance.

CFR — Cost and Freight (Named Destination Port)

The seller pays the costs and freight necessary to bring the goods to Karachi port, while buyer procures local marine insurance cover.

Seller 65%
Buyer 35%
Hunain's Operational Scope: Arranges local insurance cover before transit begins and processes customs assessment immediately upon vessel arrival notice.

EXW — Ex Works (Named Place of Delivery)

The buyer bears all costs and risks involved in taking the goods from the seller’s premises all the way to final delivery.

Seller 10%
Buyer 90%
Hunain's Operational Scope: Organizes origin pickup, overseas customs export declaration, international transport, and end-to-end import clearance.
